# Bubble Solution

This document contains recipes and testing notes for homemade bubble solution, developed as an alternative to commercial bubble solutions.

## Finetuned Recipe

- Fold in the Dawn, trying not to create bubbles
    - Mix together slowly, stirring to let it hydrate
        - Mix and give a few minutes to Disolve
            - 1500g Water (warm Tap)
            - 5g Baking Powder
        - Mix into a slurry in a separate cup
            - 75g of Glycerin
            - 1g of Guar Gum
    - 150g of Dawn
