# Bubble Solution

This document contains recipes of a homemade bubble solution.

## Recipe

### Ingredients

- 1500g Distilled Water
- 150g of dish soap (Dawn Free and Clear)
- 75g of Glycerin
- 5g Baking Powder
- 1g of Guar Gum or Xanthan Gum

### Procedure

1. Mix the water and the baking powder in a large jug
2. In a separate bowl, mix together the glycerin and guar gum to make a slurry
    - The guar gum is hydrophobic. You need to mix it with the glycerin first
3. Slowly mix the water in with the glycerin solution, stirring throughout
4. Finally add the dish soap.  Mix it slowly as to not generate too many bubbles.
5. Let the solution sit for 12 hours before use

## History

I was originally planning on buying the SunSquad bubble solution from Target. However, the large 1-gallon jugs were not in stock.  This drove me to create a solution myself. 

### Initial Recipe

To bootstrap my recipe, I looked up the Safety Data Sheet for the SunSquad Solution

- SDS for SunSquad bubble solution: <https://archpdfs.lps.org/Chemicals/BubbleSolution.pdf>

This datasheet contains a ratio of the 3 main ingredients. 

- 85% Water
- 10% Dish Soap (regular blue Dawn)
- 5% Glycerin (Use pure vegetable glycerin from Amazon)

This initial attempt was OK, but not nearly as good as SunSquad.  It was
difficult to form large bubbles and once they were formed, they didnt last very
long. They also popped "slowly", like they were slowly tearing while the
SunSquad bubbles popped very quickly, implying the bubble structure was
tighter.

### Fragrance

The classic blue Dawn has a very recognizable smell.  Evern at 10% dilution, it
is very strong. When a bubble pops near you, it is immediately noticeable.

I did an experiment switching to the Dawn Free and Clear.  The smell was
non-existent and the bubble performance was the same in my testing.

### Addition of Guar Gum

I noticed that the Homemade bubble solution was very watery compared to
SunSquad which was more syrupy and viscus. I tried playing with the ratios of
Dawn and Glycerin but it didnt seem to make much of a difference.

It was suggested that I try Guar Gum. This is commonly used to thicken sauces
when cooking. It generates long polymers in the solution which make the bubbles
stronger. A tiny amount made a HUGE difference in the thickness of the bubble
solution. 

In my testing, this made a huge improvement.  I was able to make massive
bubbles.

I noticed that the bubble still didn't pop as quickly as SunSquad.

### Addition of Baking Powder

From what i've read, this is supposed to buffer the pH of the solution and give
the bubbles even more strength.  I did notice an improvement, but it wasn't as
massive as the Guar Gum addition.  I decided to keep it in since it is a common
household produce that seems to help.
